RTSKG Ontology: Description back to ToC

The RTSKG ontology is the unified schema of the Rail Transit Station Knowledge Graph (RTSKG), a dataset that explicitly models the spatial and semantic interactions among different kinds of urban entities. It defines ten classes and nineteen relations.
